Why review the plan first

The cheapest time to change a home is before it exists.

Once walls are up, every correction becomes a site job. On paper, it is an afternoon of thinking. A review puts your decisions in the right order, at the right time.

Shift an entrance or door
On paperRedraw it and compare two options.
Once builtBreak, reframe, re-plaster and repaint.
Reposition a kitchen or bathroom
On paperMove a block and check how rooms relate.
Once builtRedo plumbing, tiling and waterproofing.
Open up a cramped centre
On paperShorten a partition and see the route clear.
Once builtLive with it, or open it up later.

Reading a plan

Zone by zone, with the reason for each note.

Traditional Vastu maps a home onto a simple grid of zones, each linked with an element. The centre, the Brahmasthan, is traditionally kept open and light.

A review reads these associations alongside daylight, ventilation and how your family lives, so every note comes with its why.

  • North-east: water
  • South-east: fire
  • South-west: earth
  • North-west: air
  • Centre: space

Simplified and illustrative. Every plan is read on its own terms.

FAQs

Can Vastu be addressed without breaking walls?

Often we can start with furniture placement, room use and how people move through the space. A review shows what is feasible in your layout and where fixed features limit your options.

My drawings are still being developed. Can I enquire?

Yes. Message us anytime. The review itself begins once the plan is nearly final and dimensioned, while changes are still easy.

What if I have more than one floor or a larger plan?

Share the area and number of floors when you enquire. Additional floors, larger plans and major revisions are quoted separately before work begins.

What is outside the review?

Structural or code checks, approvals, working drawings, revised floor plans or layout redesign (quoted separately), site supervision, complete interior design and unlimited revisions.

What does the review cost?

It is a fixed fee for one residential floor plan up to approx. 2,500 sq. ft. built-up area. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share it right away. Larger plans are quoted on enquiry.

Is it online only?

The review is online, in English or Hindi. Site visits can be arranged for an additional fee, quoted on enquiry.

How do I book?

Send a WhatsApp enquiry. We share the fee and confirm scope, availability and timeline, then you pay by UPI in full before work begins.

Is my plan kept confidential?

Yes. Plans, photos and personal details are shared only with the people working on your review.

Have a plan you’d like us to look at?

Tell us what you’re planning and where you’d like a second opinion. A message is all it takes to start.
